# TPC-H queries — attribution and fair use

The 22 queries in this directory are derived from the query definitions in the
TPC-H specification (v3.0.1), published by the
[Transaction Processing Performance Council (TPC)](https://www.tpc.org/).
"TPC-H" and "TPC Benchmark" are trademarks of the TPC. The TPC-H specification
and its copyright are the TPC's.

**These are not TPC-H results.** They are derived from TPC-H, they are not
audited or reviewed by the TPC, and they are not comparable to published TPC-H
results. Under the TPC's
[fair use policy](https://www.tpc.org/TPC_Documents_Current_Versions/pdf/Fair_Use_Quick_Reference_v1.0.0.pdf)
the name "TPC-H" is used here to identify the workload the queries derive from,
not to claim a TPC result.

**Changes made.**
- Each substitution parameter is an `@NAME@` token, filled from the sets in
  `harness/tpch_params.py` (the spec's validation values, and the values the
  daily suite runs).
- Table names are qualified at run time with the corpus being read.
- Q13 and Q15 deviate from the specification text because of engine limits;
  the reasons are in the header of each query file.
- The data is generated by a TPC-H data generator and rewritten into Parquet.
  Its free-text columns do not match other generators' output (see
  `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`).

**Purpose.** These queries are used to track the Opteryx engine's performance
over time, to identify regressions, confirm improvements, and demonstrate that
the engine is not over-fitted to any one benchmark. See the repository README.
No comparison with published TPC results is made or intended.
